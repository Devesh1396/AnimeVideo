# Veo prompts: footage C1–C7

For the Giane Future Gurukull CET reel (storyboard v3). One prompt per clip from the shot list.
Generate each clip, download it, and upload to `source/footage/` as `C1a.mp4`, `C1b.mp4` and so on (2–3 takes per clip if you can).

## Settings for every clip

- **Aspect ratio:** Portrait / 9:16. Set it in the generator's settings; don't rely on the prompt text alone.
- **Length:** 8 s (Veo's default). We only use 2–4 s of each clip.
- **Quality:** 1080p if your plan offers it.
- **Audio:** ignored. The reel has its own VO, music and sound effects. If there's an option to turn audio off, turn it off.
- **Watermark:** the brief says no visible watermarks. Some Veo plans and apps add a visible mark; check before you download. (Every Veo video also carries Google's invisible SynthID mark. That's fine.)
- **Continuity:** C3, C4, C5 and C7 show the same boy. Generate C3 first, pick the best take, and use a still from it as the **reference image / first frame** for C4, C5 and C7 (Flow calls this "Ingredients" or "Frames to video"). C1 and C6 can be any children.
- **If Veo refuses a prompt** because it shows a child: keep the same scene but frame it from behind, over the shoulder or on the hands only. Those framings work for C2, C3 and C6 because the storyboard blurs or duotones them.

## Before the reel runs as an ad

- Meta may show an "AI info" label on photorealistic AI-generated video, and its ad rules on AI content can change. Check Meta's current advertising policies before launch.
- Check each take for AI artefacts: hands and fingers, pencils merging into fingers, warped desks, garbled "text" on paper or boards. Reject any take that has them, even in the blurred clips.
- Filming at the centres is still the most authentic option. If you do film, get written consent from a parent for every child who can be identified.
